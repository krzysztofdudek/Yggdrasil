Adds the pure engine behind the advise feed. One half turns the graph's live attention signals into stable, evidence-bound nominations: each carries a canonical snapshot of exactly the evidence it rests on, hashed so that a decision made against it stops applying the instant that evidence changes. The other half folds the recorded decisions over those nominations to decide what an operator sees now versus what stays hidden — a dismissed item stays hidden while its evidence holds, a deferred item returns on or after its date, a closed item stays hidden, and any item whose evidence has moved returns as new. It keeps no clock and touches no filesystem of its own: the date is injected and file-derived inputs are passed in, so the same inputs always yield the same feed, and it never reads or writes a verdict.
