## [2026-09-06T17:57:05.834Z]
There is now more than one kind of judge, and both must be judging the same thing. The package a judge receives and the identity a verdict is stored under are therefore assembled in exactly one place: assembled twice they would drift, and a verdict recorded against a package nobody else can reproduce is bound to nothing at all. Assembly is kept free of judgement and of policy — it reads, resolves and hashes, and it neither calls a judge nor decides whether the result is allowed to be sent — so the two callers can differ in what they do with the package while agreeing completely on what it is. Its only failures are the ones where there is no package: a declared reference that cannot be read, and a companion or waiver marker that cannot be resolved. Substituting empty content in either case would hash a judgement onto inputs nobody ever saw.
